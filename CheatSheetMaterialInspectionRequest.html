MATERIAL INSPECTION REQUEST

What is a Material Inspection Request?

Material Inspection Requests (MIR) In construction and manufacturing, ensuring the quality and compliance of materials before they are incorporated into a project is crucial. One of the key processes for this is the Material Inspection Request (MIR).

The Requirements For A Material Inspection Request:
  • Forms Coming From The Consulting Company
  • First Page Of The Approved Material Submittal
  • Delivery Notes From Our Warehouse Team
  • Images Or Images Showing The Material
